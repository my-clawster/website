Integrations, audit, and health

Enterprise adds the surfaces that help operators trust the platform in production.

Integrations​

Use integrations to manage provider-facing configuration and connectivity.

Audit​

Audit records make admin changes explainable after the fact.

Health​

Health combines readiness findings across:

  • offers and product offerings
  • provider lanes
  • mappings
  • bindings
  • integrations
  • running claws

The goal is not just to show raw data. It is to shorten the path from problem detection to repair.
