# Offers and business promises

Offers define the customer-facing package. Business promises define the service guarantees that travel with it.

## Offers

An offer is how a service is positioned and exposed to users.

Examples:

- Starter
- Pro
- Scale
- Enterprise

## Product offerings

Product offerings connect an offer to a product specification and the real service model behind it.

## Business promises

Business promises hold service guarantees such as support or SLA expectations.

## Operator goal

Keep the commercial story aligned with what the platform can actually provision.
