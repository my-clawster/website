# Provider management

Provider management is where Enterprise turns provider support into an operator workflow instead of a hidden implementation detail.

## Main jobs

- review installed provider plug-ins
- run or inspect staged discovery
- approve or reject discovery proposals
- manage provider lanes
- confirm readiness and health

## Discovery model

Enterprise does not auto-trust new provider discovery results. Operators can stage, review, and approve them before they become part of the usable platform surface.

## Good operator habits

- approve deliberately
- keep lanes clearly named
- verify mappings after discovery
- confirm integration health before exposing new choices to users
