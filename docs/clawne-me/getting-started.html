Getting started with Clawne Me

The fastest path is a narrow Clawne you can test in the same session you create it.

What to decide before you start​

You only need one thing before your first Clawne: a single sentence describing what you want it to help with.

Good examples:

  • "Help me triage and draft replies to my inbox"
  • "Help me do deep research on topics I am exploring"
  • "Keep track of progress across my active projects"
  • "Help me draft and review content for my side project"

Vague examples to avoid on a first Clawne:

  • "Help me with everything"
  • "Be my general assistant"

Broad Clawnes are harder to evaluate and harder to improve. Make it narrow first.

The shortest path to your first Clawne​

  1. Create your account and sign in
  2. Choose New Clawne
  3. Enter your one-sentence goal
  4. Accept or adjust the suggested role, name, and tone
  5. Skip memory and tools for now — you can add them later
  6. Hit Launch and start testing with real prompts

How to know if it is working​

After five real prompts, you should be able to answer:

  • Does the response stay in the lane you intended?
  • Does the tone feel right for how you want to be helped?
  • Are there obvious things it is getting wrong?

If yes, you have a working first Clawne. If not, the next page explains how to fix it.
